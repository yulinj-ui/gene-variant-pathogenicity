## 如何本地审查（无需安装）

解压后将工作目录切换到 gene-variant-pathogenicity：

```bash
python3 scripts/validate_package.py
python3 scripts/run_offline_tests.py
python3 scripts/genebe_fields.py 'NM_000001.1:c.28C>T' --fixture tests/fixtures/sources.json
python3 scripts/genebe_fields.py 'NM_000001.1:c.28C>T' --fixture tests/fixtures/sources.json --card
python3 scripts/score.py tests/fixtures/score-reviewed.json
```

最后两个 fixture 都是合成测试；规则 source/reviewer 为 test-harness，不是医学审定。可从任意目录用绝对脚本/输入路径运行。联网必须显式 --online，Worker 必须显式 --worker；此阶段无需联网。
